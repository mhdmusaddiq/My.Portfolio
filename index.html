Working principles

The standards every
project should follow.

Principles I use while planning, developing and reviewing software and connected-system ideas.

01
Understand the real problem before choosing the technology.

The device, framework or AI tool is secondary to the user need and operating environment.

Engineering principle
02
Every module should have a clear reason to exist.

Endpoints, database tables, sensors and screens must connect to a specific system requirement.

Product principle
03
Complex technology should still feel simple to use.

Good engineering hides complexity behind clear interfaces, predictable flows and reliable behavior.
